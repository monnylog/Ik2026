Sponsorship follow-up agent. Queries Notion for overdue opportunities, drafts Gmail emails using Gemini API, logs all actions to the Notion Sync Log. Bridges the gap between pipeline data and human outreach.

Google Apps Script Flows
6 automation flows — Flows A–D active, E–F optional
Flow File Trigger Direction Purpose
A flow-a-notion-to-sheets.gs Every 4h Notion → Sheets Mirrors Opportunities + Activities to Google Sheets. Idempotent upsert by notion_page_id.
B flow-b-sheets-to-notion.gs Every 6h Sheets → Notion Writes ONLY computed fields back: confidence_pct, projected_value, priority_rank. Never touches protected fields.
C flow-c-notion-to-supabase.gs Every 4h Notion → Supabase Syncs Stage = "Confirmed" opportunities to ik26_sponsors. Syncs related orgs to ik26_orgs.
D flow-d-daily-digest.gs Daily 8AM PT + every 4h Notion → Gmail Daily digest email to monica.istorya@gmail.com. Stage change alerts for "Confirmed" and "In negotiation".
E opt. flow-e-gmail-to-activities.gs Every 15min Gmail → Notion Auto-creates Activity records when known contacts reply. Scoped to IK26 Gmail label only.
F opt. flow-f-calendar-milestones.gs Every 30min Calendar ↔ Notion Two-way sync between Google Calendar events tagged [IK26] and Notion Milestones.
