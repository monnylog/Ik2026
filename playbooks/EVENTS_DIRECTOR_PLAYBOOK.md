## Welcome to Isang Kusina 2026

This playbook is your operational source of truth for IK26. It compiles the staffing needs, boundaries, and system tools you will use to manage the floor and the kitchen on event day.

Your primary objective is to protect the founders' (Monica and Walbert) capacity to hold the creative vision and guest experience. You are the operational shield.

---

## 1. Your Scope & Boundaries

As the F&B / Events Director, you are the final operational authority on the floor.

### You Own:
- **BOH Coordination:** Liaising with Dio and Justin (Kitchen Leads) to ensure prep, plating, and pacing are on schedule.
- **FOH Management:** Directing Griselle (FOH Staffing) and the floor team (servers, runners, bar, expo).
- **Beverage Integration:** Coordinating with Cy (Beverage Director) to ensure drink pairings align with course pacing.
- **Vendor Logistics:** Managing load-in, load-out, and day-of vendor troubleshooting.
- **Crisis Management:** You are the first point of escalation for any operational, staffing, or equipment issue.

### The Founders Will NOT Do:
*Do not escalate these items to Monica or Walbert on event day.*
- Manage kitchen or BOH logistics
- Direct FOH staff
- Handle vendor arrivals or load-in coordination
- Troubleshoot staffing gaps on the floor
- Manage ingredient or equipment issues
- Direct kitchen prep or plating
- Manage bar or beverage service
- Handle vendor payments or invoices on the day

### When to Escalate to Founders:
- Guest experience emergencies (VIP issues, major hospitality failures)
- Chef relationship or narrative moments that require their presence
- Unplanned creative or communications decisions
- Major documentary/film coordination issues (liaise with Ayce/JJ first)

## 4. System Tools & Agents

IK26 runs on a custom tech stack. You do not need to build it, but you need to know how to read it.

**The IK26 App (Figma Make)**
You have `leadership` access. This gives you view/edit rights to:
- **Event Schedule:** The minute-by-minute run of show.
- **Menu / Courses:** Live updates on chef dish submissions and dietary flags.
- **Team Deploy:** The live roster of who is on the floor.
- **Mission Control:** A dashboard of critical alerts and blockers.

**The AI Agents**
The system uses AI agents to handle repetitive tasks. You will see their outputs in the app's `activity_feed`.
- **Salo-Salo:** Tracks chef travel, lodging, and onboarding. If a chef is missing a flight, Salo-Salo will flag it.
- **Damdam:** Monitors chef wellness (weekly pulse checks). If a chef is stressed, Damdam flags it so you can adjust their load.
- **Bantay:** The system watchdog. It provides a weekly synthesis of all chef statuses. Read this every Monday.
