**NEW FIELDS / STEPS for Chef Onboarding:**

1. **Headshot Upload** — Add a step where chefs can upload a professional headshot photo. Label it warmly: "Share a photo of yourself" with helper text like "This helps our team recognize and welcome you when you arrive." Include drag-and-drop or click-to-upload with image preview. Accept jpg/png, max 5MB.

2. **Additional File Upload** — An optional file upload area: "Anything else you'd like to share with the team?" with helper text: "Recipes, mood boards, plating references, dietary notes, ingredient lists — whatever helps us prepare for your vision." Allow multiple file types (pdf, jpg, png, doc). Optional field.

3. **Logistical / Travel Questions** — Add a logistics step with these fields:
   - "Where are you traveling from?" (city, state/country)
   - "Preferred airport for arrival" (dropdown or text)
   - "Arrival date & time" (date picker)
   - "Departure date & time" (date picker)
   - "Do you have any accessibility needs we should prepare for?" (open text, optional)
   - "Dietary preferences or allergies" (so the team can accommodate during their stay)
   - "Emergency contact name & number"
   - "Is there anything that would make your stay more comfortable?" (open text — e.g., hotel preferences, quiet workspace, early mornings, etc.)
   - "T-shirt size" (for event merch/uniforms)
   - "Preferred pronouns" (text field, optional)

4. **Collaboration Preferences** — A section that gently asks:
   - "How do you prefer to communicate during prep? (text, call, email, voice note)"
   - "What does your ideal kitchen workflow look like?" (open text)
   - "Is there anything about your cooking style or process that would be helpful for the team to know ahead of time?" (open text)
   - "Would you like a walkthrough of the kitchen space before the event?" (yes/no)

**DESIGN & TONE PHILOSOPHY (apply throughout, do NOT label it as trauma-informed):**

- All language should feel like a warm invitation, never a requirement or interrogation. Use "share" instead of "submit", "let us know" instead of "report", "we'd love to hear" instead of "you must provide"
- Every required field should have a clear, gentle reason WHY it's being asked (helper text explaining how the info helps the team support them)
- No field should feel like surveillance or gatekeeping. Frame everything around care and preparation: "so we can...", "to make sure you feel...", "this helps us..."
- Avoid any language that implies hierarchy — no "your host", "management requires", "you will be assigned to". Instead use collaborative language: "the team", "your fellow chefs", "together we'll", "our shared kitchen"
- Progress through onboarding should feel like a conversation, not a form. Use transitions like "Almost there" or "One more thing" between steps
- Include a final confirmation screen that feels celebratory and personal: "Welcome to the kitchen, [Name]" with warmth, not corporate
- Make sure the tone never positions Istorya above the chef — they are honored collaborators, not vendors or contractors
- Remove any existing language that could read as transactional or hierarchical

- Display the chosen avatar + their first name in community spaces, chat, and the chef roster
